How to play

Desktop

  • WASD or arrows: steer your cycle
  • Space or Shift: engage turbo boost
  • P or Esc: pause (single-player) · M: mute

Touch

  • Swipe in any cardinal direction to steer
  • Tap anywhere to start or restart from menu

Rules & Tactics

  • Crossing any light trail or hitting outer walls causes an explosion
  • Trap your opponent into tight corridors to force a crash
  • First to 3 round wins claims match victory
