How to play

Desktop

  • Mouse — move the paddle
  • AD or arrows — move the paddle
  • Click or Space — launch the ball
  • P or Esc — pause · M — mute

Touch

  • Drag anywhere to move the paddle
  • Tap to launch the ball or start a run

Scoring

  • Top rows are worth more points than the bottom ones
  • Clearing every brick advances the level and adds a row
  • Losing the ball costs a life; three lives per run
