How to play

Desktop

  • WASD or arrows — slide all tiles
  • Enter or R — start / restart
  • M — mute

Touch

  • Swipe in any direction to slide the board
  • Tap to start or restart from the menu

Boards

  • Classic — 4×4, target 2048
  • Compact — 3×3, target 512
  • Grand — 5×5, target 4096
  • Merging scores the combined value; each board keeps its own best
