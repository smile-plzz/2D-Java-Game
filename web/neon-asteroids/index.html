How to play

Desktop

  • W or ↑: engage forward thrusters
  • AD or ←→: rotate ship
  • Space: fire blaster cannons
  • Shift or H: emergency hyperspace jump
  • P or Esc: pause · M: mute

Touch

  • Left side: hold to engage thrusters
  • Right side: tap to fire lasers

Tactics

  • Large asteroids break into two medium ones; medium break into two small ones
  • Manage your inertia: tap thrust gently so you don't drift uncontrollably
  • Screen edges wrap around seamlessly for both you and asteroids
