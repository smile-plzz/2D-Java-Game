How to play

Desktop

  • Mouse — move your paddle (left side)
  • WS or arrows — move your paddle
  • P or Esc — pause · M — mute

Touch

  • Drag anywhere to move your paddle
  • Tap to start or restart from the menu

Scoring

  • First to 7 points wins the match
  • Where the ball meets your paddle steers the return angle
  • Rallies speed up the ball slightly with every hit
