How to play

Desktop

  • Mouse — move your paddle (left side)
  • WS or arrows — move your paddle
  • P or Esc — pause · M — mute

Touch

  • Drag anywhere to move your paddle
  • Tap to start or restart from the menu

Modes

  • Classic — first to 7
  • Blitz — short paddles, fast serve, first to 5
  • Multiball — three balls at once, first to 11
  • Where the ball meets your paddle steers the return angle, and rallies speed it up
