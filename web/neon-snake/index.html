How to play

Desktop

  • WASD or arrows — steer
  • P or Esc — pause · M — mute
  • Enter or R — start / restart

Touch

  • Swipe in any direction to turn
  • Tap to start or restart from the menu

Modes

  • Classic — lethal walls, 10 a pellet
  • Wrap — edges tunnel through, faster, 12 a pellet
  • Maze — blocks in the arena, 15 a pellet
  • Each mode keeps its own best score
