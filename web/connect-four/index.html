How to play

Desktop

  • Mouse — hover a column, click to drop
  • AD or arrows — move the column marker
  • Space or Enter — drop your disc

Touch

  • Tap a column to drop your disc there
  • Tap to start or restart from the menu

Scoring

  • Connect four discs — horizontal, vertical or diagonal — to win
  • Win streak counts consecutive match wins against the AI
  • The AI plays a full board search, not a random spread
