How to play

Desktop

  • Mouse - hover a column, click to drop
  • AD or arrows - move the column marker
  • Space or Enter - drop your disc

Touch

  • Tap a column to drop your disc there
  • Tap to start or restart from the menu

Online Play

  • Click Play online on the menu
  • Host creates a room and shares the 6-character code or link
  • Guest enters the code and joins the match directly
